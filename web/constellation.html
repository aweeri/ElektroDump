Every dot is one demodulated symbol, scaled by the symbol RMS. A locked PSK/Pm-BPSK link shows a tight cloud on the axis: two lobes for BPSK, four for OQPSK. A ring means the carrier loop is not tracking, a fuzzy blob means the symbol timing is off.
